Why Credify Exists

Building trust in an age of synthetic media

Generative AI has made it trivial to create convincing images and videos. That's powerful for creativity, but dangerous for trust. It's getting harder for people and platforms to quickly answer a simple question: "How real is this?"

Credify was built to treat media authenticity as a security signal, not a visual judgment. Instead of asking humans to squint at pixels, Credify provides a machine-readable probability that a piece of media was AI-generated, plus context from Content Credentials when they're present.

"In a world where seeing is no longer believing, we need tools that can verify what our eyes cannot."

— The Credify Team

What Credify Aims to Unlock

  • Faster Triage

    For Trust & Safety teams reviewing user-generated content. Reduce review time with automated risk scoring.

  • Upstream Fraud Signals

    For marketplaces, fintech, and identity flows. Detect synthetic media before it enters your pipeline.

  • Better Moderation Tooling

    For social products that want "authenticity aware" features. Build trust into your platform's DNA.

  • Education & Awareness

    For journalists, educators, and everyday users learning how AI media behaves. Understand the technology that shapes our world.

Our Approach

Credify combines three specialized streams in a conservative gate ensemble. The pipeline routes inputs through a C2PA cryptographic signature check, a SigLIP-2 and U-Net transposed decoder for semantic anomalies and localization, and a 2D Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) log-magnitude classifier to identify upsampling noise artifacts.

This dual-stream architecture operates locally on GPU infrastructure, achieving high validation throughput with an average processing latency under 165 milliseconds.

Built By

Credify was built by Daras Bhullar as a production-ready demonstration of AI-powered media authenticity detection. The platform is designed to be integrated into existing workflows, not to exist as a standalone demo.
