Privacy

This portal is a prototype and focuses on the detection behaviour rather than full legal terms. The notes below describe how the demo handles your uploads.

How Uploads Are Used

  • Your images and videos are used only to run the Credify detection models inside this server.
  • No attempt is made to identify people in your media.
  • Content Credentials (C2PA) are read only to understand the file's provenance claims.

Storage

  • All uploaded media is processed in-memory and discarded immediately upon response completion.
  • This demo does not write uploads to disk or persist them long-term.

If You Integrate Credify Yourself

If Credify is integrated into your own product stack, you can apply your own logging, retention, and compliance policies around uploads and scores. This portal is meant to showcase the detection behaviour, not provide legal advice.
